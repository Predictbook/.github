# Predictbook

An independent intelligence desk for prediction markets. We track Polymarket, Kalshi, and the wider ecosystem on odds, volume, and timing, not headlines.

## Open-source lists

- **[Awesome Prediction Market Analytics Tools](https://github.com/Predictbook/awesome-prediction-market-analytics-tools)**: a growing list of tools for tracking prediction market data, including analytics dashboards, trading terminals, odds aggregators, and whale tracking. Built a tool? Open a PR.
- **[Awesome Prediction Market Follows](https://github.com/Predictbook/awesome-prediction-market-follows)** (work in progress): the analysts, traders, platforms, and outlets worth following.
